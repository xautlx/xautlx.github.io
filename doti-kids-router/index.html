使用安心Peace of mind

只管时间，不看内容Time, not content

守护的是节奏和习惯，不是监视。It's about rhythm and habits — not surveillance.

不看上网内容No snooping

只管网速和时长，不看孩子上网的内容。Only speed and time are managed — never what your kids are looking at.

在家管理不经过云端At home, no cloud in between

在家时手机直接和路由器通信。开着远程管理时，云端每天只收到前一天的上网时长，不含上网内容。At home your phone talks to the router directly. With remote management on, the cloud only gets yesterday's screen time each day — no browsing content.

程序开源可查Open for inspection

路由器上的程序全部开源，懂技术的人都能检查它做了什么。The router software is fully open source — anyone can check what it does.

WiFi 密码不上云WiFi passwords stay off the cloud

家长和孩子的 WiFi 密码只存在家里的路由器和你们自己的设备上，不上传云端。Parents' and kids' WiFi passwords stay on your home router and your own devices — never uploaded to the cloud.

先用，呵护孩子零门槛Use first — no barrier to caring for your kids

在家管理一直免费；远程管理全家共用，满意了再付。Managing at home is always free; remote management is shared by the family — pay only if it's worth it.
